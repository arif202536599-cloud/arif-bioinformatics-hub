App name: **Arif Bio Hub**

Short description: Learn bioinformatics and explore protein-to-gene reference evidence.

Full description:

Arif Bio Hub brings bioinformatics learning and reference-based protein exploration into one app.

Follow learning pathways, browse computational biology tools, save bookmarks, take quizzes, track progress, and keep research notes on your device. Learning content and the labelled synthetic demonstration can open offline.

The Protein-to-Central-Dogma Explorer accepts pasted amino-acid sequences or protein FASTA files. Search across organisms or specify an organism, review NCBI BLASTp candidates, and choose reference proteins for linked gene, transcript, coding-sequence and genomic evidence where databases provide it. Inspect alignment evidence, translation comparisons and annotated gene structure. Share Excel, FASTA, JSON and figure outputs through Android.

A protein cannot uniquely reconstruct its original DNA. Identical proteins may occur in multiple genes or organisms. Database matches are reference evidence, not proof of the origin of a sample. RNA-seq expression requires separate experimental data and is not inferred from protein input. Missing or ambiguous database information is reported explicitly.

Online searches require internet and send selected sequences through the developer's backend to NCBI after your confirmation. The app uses a local guest session. No advertising SDK is included. External videos and source databases open through browser links.
